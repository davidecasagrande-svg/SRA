Figure C-1 · Slope geometry — descriptive terminology
Ideal section through a cut (left) and a fill embankment (right). Scroll sideways to see the whole section.

Dimensions in metres. Angles in degrees or as H:V ratio (the latter is normally used in construction drawings and is preferred for describing constructed slopes).
Cut batters — number consecutively from the base of the cut and label L or R (e.g. B1L, B2L).
Shoulder / verge — see Road Design Guide section 3.5. The Clear Fall Zone is different to the Clear Zone in the Design Guide.
Embankment is the structure above the natural surface. Fill is the material making up the embankment.
Stepped fills and dual carriageways (raised or depressed median) follow the same naming — see the Guide, Figure C-1.
